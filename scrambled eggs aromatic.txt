Scrambled Eggs Aromatic recipe

Three eggs. 
Tablespoon of salted butter. 
Quarter of a cup of milk or a little more. 
Quarter of a teaspoon of tarragon. 
Quarter of a teaspoon of poppy seeds.
Quality fresh ground pepper to taste. 

In a bowl, mix all ingredients together until a consistent colour.
Cook in a frying pan on stove, scraping the bottom of the pan clear every 30 seconds also, use medium low heat, until moisture has evaporated (not liquid).
